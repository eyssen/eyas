# Sponsors

EYAS is built and maintained by one person, and it is developed with the same AI
models it orchestrates. That inference is the project's largest running cost —
sponsorship is what keeps it paid for.

**[Become a sponsor →](https://github.com/sponsors/eyssen)**

Everything EYAS ships stays [MIT-licensed](LICENSE) and self-hostable, sponsored
or not. Sponsorship buys the pace, not the access. It is not a support contract:
no tier includes a response-time or fix-time guarantee.

## Current goal

## Getting listed

Reply to the welcome message after sponsoring with the name you want shown, and —
for the Team and Partner tiers — an SVG or transparent PNG logo plus the URL it
should link to. Listings are updated with each release.

Logo placement is editorial: sponsorship does not imply endorsement in either
direction, and a logo may be declined or removed if it would misrepresent the
project. See [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md) for what may and may not be called EYAS.
