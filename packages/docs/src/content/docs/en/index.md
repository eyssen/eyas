**EYAS** is a self-hosted personal AI platform: agents, memory, board, multi-channel communication, and more — on your machine, under your control.

This docs is for **operators and users**. Deep architecture and contributor specs live in the repository `docs/` tree (see [Architecture pointer](/docs/en/reference/architecture/)).
