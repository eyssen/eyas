**nuqmeH.** nab qumwI'mey ghom wa' vIH/tIn qumDaq. Dachen, Dachel, pagh ghoqwI' ghItlh; ghop, qumDaq, pagh AI DachoH; chovnatlh Dacher 'ej tayqeq, ja'chuq leghmeH. teDwI' Segh Claude Design 'oH; Qap EYAS 'oH.

## ghorgh yIlo'

- UI, landing, print, pagh deck nabbogh 'ej EYASDaq DaneH, Hur jan neHbe'.
- ghoqwI' ponglu'bogh qumwI'mey (tokens, components, page) laDnIS, qach QIjbe'.
- noblu'bogh Claude Design qum yIchel, pagh PNG/PDF yInobHa'.
- ja'chuq pagh Qu' Hoch mIwDaq qum jaHnIS.

## motlh mIw

1. **nabmey** yIpoSmoH 'em tlheghDaq (**De'**) — He `/design`.
2. pong yIghItlh 'ej **New** yIchu' (pagh noblu'bogh qum HTML **Import**).
3. qumDaq, **Source**Daq, pagh **AI** pa'Daq yIchoH. **Save** (wa' chovnatlh toD Hoch).
4. ja'chuqDaq **nabmey** Degh yIlo' tayqeqmeH. ghoqwI' 'ay'mey SuqlaH; qum chaw' tetlhDaq nargh.

nab: wa' vIHlaHbogh tInmoHlaHbogh qumDaq qumwI'mey ghom. Hoch qumwI' `.dc.html`
teywI' 'oH; `canvas.json` qum: nuqDaq Qam Hoch, nuq navDaq ghom, 'ej poSDI' chu'
nuq legh. mIllogh qumDaq ratlh, pongDaj lo'taHvIS.

teywI' Segh: Claude Design Segh 'oH. vaj pa' chenmoHlu'bogh qum naDev
lIHlaHlu' 'ej cha'lu'; naDev ngeHlu'bogh qum pa' chenqa'moHlu'. EYAS mob
vum lo' cha'meH — cha' jan teywI' Segh boch, De' boChbe'.

## nab chenmoHmeH

`/design` navDaq pong yIghItlh, **chu'** yIQIp. wa' qumwI' taghwI' Suq, choHmeH.

**lIH**: cha'lu'bogh nab qum nav naQ HTML laj. nav 'ay' polbogh pat tepDaq
ratlhbogh 'ej navDaq ratlhbe'bogh: lajQo'lu'. 'oH mIllogh: poSchoHwI' wa'DIch
Doch neH, 'ej lIHDI' tugh Doch qan Suq.

HuchwI' je chenmoHlaH. HuchwI' chenmoHbogh Hoch: SoH choHmey rap juvlu'.

## qumDaq vIHtaHghach

'em yIQam. tlhe'wI' vIHmoH, **Shift**+tlhe'wI' tlhopDaq, **Ctrl/⌘**+tlhe'wI'
tInmoH — 'uch tInmoHwI' 'oS: 'oS bIngDaq Doch ratlh. **Fit**: navDaq Hoch 'ang.

vIHtaHghach: qumwI'mey *retlhDaq* vum, chaj patlhDaq vumbe'. qumwI' 'oH
botlhbogh Sor, 'ej 'uch wanI'mey chaj pol — vumbogh Doch vumtaHmoH.

qum navmey law' ghajDI', nav Dochmey nIvDaq cha'lu'.

## wa' qumwI' poSmoH

Hoch qumwI' pong retlhDaq poSwI' tu'lu' — ghap pongDaq cha'logh yIQIp. mob 'ej
Hoch legh buy'moH, 'ej **Esc** DaHjaj Daqlij chegh.

chay' poSlu' — qumwI' Doch 'oH: tagh Hoch mach chargh; buy'moHbogh qumwI' 'ach
legh 'ab 'aqroS 'ab lo'taHvIS tInmoH 'ej tlhe'moH — 'e' poQ vIHbogh 'ab nab.

## wej choHmeH mIw

**qumDaq.** **choH** yIpoSmoH 'ej Doch yIQIp. Doch SeH: ngutlh, rItlh, ghor,
veH, mIw je choH; rap 'ab ghajbogh Hoch chIm: mI' neH choHlu'. mu'mey Daq
choHlaH, qumwI' De'wI' mu'mey 'oHbe'chugh — vaj SeH 'ang, boQ SeHHa'be'.

Cmd/Ctrl+Z chIl, Shift chegh, 'ej polDI' neH pollu': polmeH wa' Doch, Qub
Hoch cha'be'.

vumbogh qumwI' chaj SeHmey pol, 'ej mung SeHDaq choHlu' — wIvDI', prototype
poQbogh QIpmey Sop.

**mungDaq.** mung SeH: qum teywI' Hoch tetlh, 'ej chaj choH.

**AI lo'taHvIS.** AI SeH yIpoSmoH, choH yIDel, yIlo'.

nuq Doch 'ej nuqvo' ghoS: polpa' qum chutmey juvlu'. Sor Hutlhbogh qumwI',
tu'lu'be'bogh teywI'Daq 'ang'eghbogh mIw De', pagh ghajbogh mIllogh Del,
ghap ghor Hutlhbogh chut ghajbogh style Doch — Hoch lajQo'lu', 'ej Doch qan
rap ratlh. wa'DIch nISDI' ghomwI', EYAS Qagh 'ang 'ej wa'logh tlhob.

Hoch chuqbogh nobwI' rap vum. EYAS Qu' nob wa' chenmoHwI' janDaq neH
ghobe' — mIw, juv, polbogh Doch je rap.

CLI nobwI'Daq 'ej qum tIn: AI choH poH law' 'oH. qaStaHvIS poH juvtaH SeH 'ej
'ang; nav Damejchugh, mevbe'. Hoch nID qonlu', vaj Qav nID 'ang SeH —
lo'lu'pu', Qapbe'pu' ('ej qatlh), pagh qumwI' taghqa'mo' mev — nav
DalaDqa'pu'chugh joq rar chIlpu'chugh je. choH qaStaHvIS, qum rapDaq cha'DIch
choH taghlaHbe'lu'.

## SeHmey

SeH Dochmey: qumwI' mob 'ang'eghbogh wIvmey. wa' choHDI', SIbI' cha'qa';
ratlhmoHDI', qumwI' tagh Doch moj.

## Doch qan

Hoch choH Doch qan 'oH — 'Iv, nuq, 'ej nuv, lIH, AI ghap. Doch qan chegh:
chu'Daq vel, vaj pagh chIl.

## qumwI'mey pong, tu'lu'meH

HuchwI'pu'lI' Hoch qum laDbe' — veb 'ay' yIlegh. tetlh laD, 'ej Hoch qumwI'
Doch ta'bogh vI'; pong QaQ ghajbogh qumwI' tu'. mu'mey:

| Doch | nuq ngaS |
|---|---|
| **tokens** | rItlh, chuq, veH — Hoch latlh Sovbogh Doch |
| **typography** | 'ab, 'ugh, ngutlh Segh |
| **components** | button, ghItlh Daq, Degh: 'ay'mey, chaj DochmeyDaq |
| **patterns** | 'ay'mey boqlu': nav, tetlh, jan SIrgh |
| **page** | nav naQ ghap ghItlhlu'bogh nav |

`canvas.json` pongvo' Doch laDlu', ghIq teywI' pongvo'. *Tokens*, *Typography*,
*Components* pong ghajbogh nab: lengmeH lugh. *Frame 1* – *Frame 5* pong
ghajbogh vagh qumwI': ghaytan poSlu'. AI chenmoHbogh nabmey mob vaj pong.

nab pat qum: puS tokens qumwI' 'ej typography qumwI' qengnIS.

## nab tayqeq

**ja'chuqDaq.** ja'chuq nIvDaq **nabmey** Degh: qum tayqeq. DeghDaq mI': 'ar
lo'lu'. SeH: Hoch nab 'ang, tayqeqlu'bogh Doch ngevmoHlu'. HuchwI'pu' je
tayqeqlaH 'ej teqlaH.

**nabDaq.** **nabmey → yIchoH** bIngDaq. nabDaq chenmoHlu'bogh ja'chuq: nab
qummey tlhej tagh, 'ej ghIq ghaH Doch — pa' teqDI', ja'chuqvetlh neH Say'moH.
nabDaq lanlu'chugh, ja'chuq chu' Suq; lanlu'be'chugh, Suqbe'. tugh nab choHDI',
ja'chuqmey tu'lu'bogh SIchbe'.

De' mung yotlhmey je rap Doch 'oH.

## tayqeqlu'bogh nab: HuchwI' legh nuq

qum legh be' — Hoch tlheDaq wa'maH SaD ngutlh 'oH. DochmeyDaj je legh be':
**maq** neH. tayqeqlu' 'e' maq nab, 'ej Hoch 'ay'Daj nuq Segh De' ngaS 'ang —
tokens (rItlh, chuq, veH), ngutlh, chenwI' ghormey, 'ay'mey boqlu'. vagh qumwI'
46 KB Odoo nab: **652 ngutlh**, 'ej tInchoHDI' nab, rap ratlh.

ghIq nuq poQ neH Suq HuchwI': `design_read` `part` tlhej — wa' 'ay' De'
lIngbogh (rItlh, ngutlh Segh), mach. `design_read` `file` tlhej — wa' qumwI'
markup naQ, markup poQlu'DI'.

**qatlh rItlhmey lanbe'lu'?** poH puS lanlu'ta'. **Hoch tlheDaq** DIl maq;
**wa'logh** neH DIl SuqwI'. cha' tlheDaq SuqwI' pIm QaQ law', 'ej SuqwI' neH
'oH DIlmeH qum tInchoHDI' tInchoHbe'bogh. Hoch 'abDaq rap mIw, vaj nab mach je
maq, lanbe'lu'.

je HuchwI' ra' maq: nab yItlha', tayqeqlu' 'e' 'ang neH ghobe' — vaj
ja'chuqvetlhDaq chenmoHbogh Doch rItlhlIj, ngutlhlIj, chenwI' ghormey je lo'.

## ngeH 'ej ghItlh

ngeH SeH: cha' Segh Doch nob.

**teywI'mey**: qum mob nob — HTML nav mobbogh, Hoch ghomwI'Daq poSlaHbogh, ghap
qumwI' nav qenglaHbogh, latlh jan lo'laHbogh.

**ghItlh**: teH ghomwI' lo'taHvIS qum cha' — wIvbogh qumwI' PNG rap ghap
cha'logh 'ab, wIvbogh qumwI' PDF, ghap Hoch qum wa' PDF.

chay' ghItlhlu' qumwI' — qumwI' Doch 'oH. **SertaHbogh** qumwI' — Doch tagh,
'ej naDev tu'lu' 'oS, ghItlhmey nav je — wa' nav neH moj, qumDaq 'ab rap.
**vIHbogh** qumwI' — De' nav, De'wI' De' — A4 ghap Letter tlhe'moH, ngeH
SeHDaq wIvbogh; nav 'ab law' 'oHbogh 'ab puS chargh, 'ej machbogh 'oH
chenmoHwI' wIvbogh 'abDaq ratlh, tInmoHlu'be'.

Hoch qum PDF: Hoch qumwI' nav mob ghaj, qumDaq laDlu'bogh mIwDaq: nav nav,
ghIq 'eqvo' bIngDaq, ghIq poSvo' nIHDaq. chaj 'ab ratlh navmey, vaj pIm
'abmey ghajbogh qumwI'mey ghajbogh ghItlhmey nav lugh moj.

ghItlhmeH EYAS retlhDaq lanlu'bogh ghomwI' poQlu'. tu'lu'be'chugh, ghItlh
Dochmey chIlmoHlu' 'ej nuq lanlu'nIS 'ang SeH. **teywI'mey** bIngDaq Hoch
vumtaH.

## pong choH 'ej Qaw'

nav nIvDaq pong yIQIp, yIghItlh, Enter. Esc chIl.

nav nIv nIHDaq veQ teywI' Degh tu'lu': Hoch nab Qaw'. wa'DIch ghel, 'ej
ghelbogh mu'mey 'ang nuq chIl: Hoch chovnatlh polbogh, 'ej Hoch ja'chuq pagh
nab ghom tayqeqlu'bogh. choHqa'laHbe'lu', 'ej veQ teywI' tu'lu'be'.
