# Stakeholder Communication

## Stakeholder Mapping
- **Power/Interest Grid:** categorize stakeholders by influence and engagement
  - High Power, High Interest → Manage Closely (regular updates, involve in decisions)
  - High Power, Low Interest → Keep Satisfied (executive summaries)
  - Low Power, High Interest → Keep Informed (newsletters, dashboards)
  - Low Power, Low Interest → Monitor (minimal communication)

## Communication Formats by Audience

### C-Level / Executives
- One-page summary with RAG status (Red/Amber/Green)
- Business impact metrics, not technical details
- Key risks and mitigation strategies
- Budget and timeline status
- Decision requests clearly stated

### Product Owners / Managers
- Sprint progress: velocity, burn-down chart
- Feature completion status with demo links
- Blockers requiring their input
- Upcoming priorities and trade-off decisions

### Development Team
- Technical details, architecture decisions
- Pull request reviews, code walkthroughs
- Sprint planning and daily standups
- Knowledge sharing sessions

### End Users
- Release notes in plain language
- Training materials and documentation
- Feedback channels and support contacts

## Communication Cadence
- Daily: standup (team internal)
- Weekly: status report (stakeholders)
- Bi-weekly: sprint review/demo (broader audience)
- Monthly: strategic review (executives)
- Quarterly: roadmap review (all stakeholders)

## Difficult Conversations
- Deliver bad news early — never hide delays
- Present problems with proposed solutions
- Use data to support your position
- Separate facts from opinions
- Document decisions and follow-up actions
